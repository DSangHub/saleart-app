How SaleArt Works

1. Write in the App

Type your message — “50% OFF”, “OPEN HOUSE”, “YARD SALE”, a price, or a QR prompt. Choose font size and style for distance readability.

2. E-ink Display Updates

First version uses proven e-ink (e-paper) panels. The app sends the text; the sign updates in seconds and holds the image with almost no power — readable in sunlight, no backlight needed.

3. Display, Erase, Reuse

The text stays visible indefinitely. When you’re done, update or clear it from the app. Same physical sign is ready for the next message — no printing, no waste.

First version: E-ink panels. Inspired by the visual magic of magnetic particle / Etch A Sketch toys, SaleArt’s initial product uses reliable, low-power e-ink technology for outdoor-ready, app-controlled sale signs. Magnetic particle versions remain a future exploration.

Three Versions

Choose the form factor that fits your sale. All versions use e-ink panels, are app-controlled, reusable, and update with one tap.

1. Standard Rectangular

Classic Etch A Sketch style frame. Bold, high-visibility rectangular display perfect for yards, driveways, and storefronts. Maximum readability at a distance.

2. Chat Bubble

Fun speech-bubble shape. App includes color selection (where supported by the e-ink panel) and a choice of fonts (bold block, condensed, playful). Great for retail, markets, and casual events.

3. AirTag Security

Available on any version: built-in or attachable AirTag-style tracker. Locate your sign if it walks away. Peace of mind for outdoor and unattended displays.

App controls everything: Type your message, pick the font, choose color options (where supported), send to the sign, and update or clear when done. Security pairing works through the same app.
