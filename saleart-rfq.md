# SaleArt.app — Short RFQ (Request for Quotation)

**Product:** Reusable app-controlled sale signs  
**First version technology:** E-ink (e-paper) panels  
**Concept:** Etch A Sketch–inspired housings with low-power, sunlight-readable displays that update from a mobile app.

## Product Versions (all use e-ink)
1. **Standard Rectangular** — Classic frame, high-visibility outdoor/yard use.
2. **Chat Bubble** — Speech-bubble shaped housing. App supports color options (where panel allows) and font choices.
3. **AirTag-style Security** — Optional built-in or clip-on tracker slot / BLE beacon on any version.

## Key Requirements
- E-ink / e-paper panel (monochrome or limited color preferred for outdoor contrast)
- App-controlled updates (NFC preferred for battery-free small units; Bluetooth or Wi-Fi acceptable)
- Image holds with near-zero power after update
- Sunlight readable, no backlight required
- Weatherproof housing suitable for outdoor use (IP65 or better preferred)
- Custom plastic housings (rectangular and chat-bubble shapes)
- Optional solar or long-life battery options for larger units
- AirTag-style tracker recess or integrated BLE module
- Scalable from small (≈4–8") to larger yard-sign sizes

## Deliverables Requested
- Unit pricing at volumes (100 / 500 / 1,000+)
- Lead time for samples and production
- Available panel sizes and color options
- Custom housing / injection-molding capability
- Connectivity options (NFC, BLE, Wi-Fi)
- Outdoor durability notes
- MOQ and sample cost
